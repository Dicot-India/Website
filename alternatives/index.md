# SCADA alternatives in India: how to choose.

A SCADA alternative is software that delivers the core SCADA job — collect data, show it, alarm on it, store it and report on it — without the cost and deployment model of a traditional enterprise system. The three common shapes are enterprise SCADA, mini-SCADA and cloud SCADA.

## The three shapes, compared

|   | Traditional enterprise SCADA | Mini-SCADA | Cloud SCADA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Typical scope** | Whole plant, many lines | One machine or line | Remote and multi-site monitoring |
| **Tag ceiling** | 10,000–100,000+ | Up to 100 | 10–20,000 per project |
| **Deployment** | On-premise servers | One Windows PC | Vendor-hosted |
| **Time to first data** | Weeks to months | Hours to days | Hours |
| **Works offline** | Yes | Yes | Partial — local buffering needed |
| **Remote access** | VPN or web client | Via cloud forwarding | Native, browser and mobile |
| **Audit trails** | Usually | Basic | Varies |
| **Licensing** | Tag + client + module | Term or perpetual | Yearly subscription |
| **IT burden** | High | Low | Low |

Ranges are typical of the category, not a specific competitor. Confirm limits with any vendor before you buy.

Five questions

## Ask these before you sign.

The answers decide more than the feature list.

1.  **How many tags will you actually need in three years?** Buy for the plant you will have, not the pilot.
2.  **What happens when the internet drops?** Control must keep running locally; only the remote view should depend on the cloud.
3.  **Who owns the historical data?** Ask for export in a standard format, not a proprietary dump.
4.  **Which protocols are supported natively?** Every unsupported driver becomes a custom integration line item.
5.  **What does year three cost?** Renewals, client licences, support and update policy matter more than the first invoice.

FAQ

## SCADA alternative questions

What is a SCADA alternative?

A SCADA alternative is software that provides the core SCADA functions — data collection, operator screens, alarms, trends, history and reports — without the cost and deployment model of a traditional enterprise SCADA. Common alternatives are mini-SCADA for single machines and cloud SCADA for remote monitoring.

Is a mini-SCADA enough for a full plant?

Often, no. A mini-SCADA such as Vision C is designed for up to 100 tags, typically one machine or a compact line. A plant with multiple lines, recipes, audit trails and long history needs an enterprise platform such as Vision S or a cloud platform such as Vision Web.

Can cloud SCADA replace on-premise SCADA?

For monitoring and alarming, usually yes. For high-speed control, safety functions or sites with unreliable connectivity, keep control on-premise and send data to the cloud. Most plants end up with a hybrid architecture.

How do I compare SCADA licensing models?

Compare the total cost over the plant's life, not the sticker price. Check tag limits, runtime versus development editions, client licences, update policy and what happens when you need 20% more tags. Ask for the renewal cost in writing.

Does Dicot replace a full MES or ERP?

No. Dicot provides the SCADA, IIoT and reporting layer between machines and business systems. It can forward data to MES, ERP or BI tools, but it is not an MES or ERP.
