## Tell us what the software is doing.

Tickets are handled in the support portal, a separate service with its own [privacy notice](https://helpdesk.dicot.tech/privacy). Please do not send plant passwords or other people's personal data in a ticket.

## What to include

-   The product and version — for example Vision S Version 2026, or your Vision C build.
-   Your licence ID, or the key label colour for Vision S.
-   The Windows version on the machine that is failing.
-   What you expected, what happened, and any error text.
-   A screenshot of the screen or alarm, if you can take one.
-   Whether production has stopped, slowed, or is still running.

## What happens after you submit.

1.  Step 01
    
    ### You open a ticket
    
    The form goes straight into the Dicot helpdesk, not a shared inbox, so the request is recorded with a number.
    
2.  Step 02
    
    ### We triage it
    
    Production stops come first. You get a reply on the ticket and a tracking link by email.
    
3.  Step 03
    
    ### Fix, patch or a call
    
    Most issues close on the ticket. If it needs a person at the plant, we arrange a call or a site visit.
