Industries

Nine industries. One data layer.

The equipment changes; the problem does not. Collect reliable data, keep it safe, show it to the right person and raise an alarm when it drifts. Here is how that looks in each industry we serve.

PharmaceuticalsTextilesFood & beverageMachine toolsWater treatmentFoundryCeramicsRailwayHVAC
Industry / 01

Pharmaceuticals

Regulated production needs more than a dashboard. Vision S records who changed what, when, and why, with audit trails and electronic signatures that hold up to an audit. Batch data is stored in PostgreSQL so quality teams can query it directly.

Built on Vision S Discuss your plant
  • 21 CFR-ready audit trails and e-signatures
  • Batch recording with multiple simultaneous batches
  • Reports with your formats and approval headers
Industry / 02

Textiles

Dyeing, spinning and processing lines fail quietly: a temperature drifts, a batch runs long, a shift loses the register. Vision C logs the process locally and Vision Web puts the line on a supervisor's phone.

Built on Vision C Discuss your plant
  • Multi-port Modbus data collection
  • Shift and batch reports
  • Alarms by WhatsApp or SMS when a line drifts
Industry / 03

Food & beverage

Temperature, pressure and time decide whether a batch is safe. Dicot software records the evidence continuously and turns it into a report that quality and customers can sign off.

Built on Vision C Discuss your plant
  • Batch-wise logging and traceability
  • Temperature and pressure alarms
  • PDF reports with logo and batch header
Industry / 04

Machine tools

Machine builders should not become software companies. Vision C gives your machine a SCADA and data logger, and Vision Web gives your customer remote visibility — under your brand.

Built on Vision C Discuss your plant
  • White-label OEM editions
  • Licensing tied to machine warranty
  • One-click cloud connection for your customer
Industry / 05

Water treatment

Water sites are remote, unmanned or both. Vision Web watches tank levels, flow, pH and conductivity, and escalates alarms to the people who can act.

Built on Vision Web Discuss your plant
  • Level, flow and quality trending
  • SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram alarms
  • Multi-site visibility from one login
Industry / 06

Foundry

Furnaces, moulding lines and cooling conveyors generate data in harsh conditions. Vision C runs on modest industrial PCs and keeps logging when the network does not.

Built on Vision C Discuss your plant
  • Local history on the plant floor
  • Furnace and cooling trend logs
  • Shift comparison reports
Industry / 07

Ceramics

Kiln cycles are long and expensive to repeat. Dicot software records the full curve, compares batches and exports the evidence for customers and process teams.

Built on Vision S Discuss your plant
  • Long-cycle trending and history
  • Batch comparison
  • Energy and temperature reporting
Industry / 08

Railway

Wayside and depot equipment is distributed across sites and often on poor networks. Vision C logs locally; Vision Web aggregates what matters centrally.

Built on Vision Web Discuss your plant
  • Distributed data collection
  • Store-and-forward architecture
  • Central alarms and reports
Industry / 09

HVAC

Comfort complaints and energy bills rarely arrive with the same owner. Vision Web tracks AHU temperatures, humidity and fan status, and alerts facilities before a space drifts.

Built on Vision Web Discuss your plant
  • AHU and room condition monitoring
  • Humidity and temperature alarms
  • Trends for energy reviews
