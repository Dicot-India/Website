How it works

Three steps, no big-bang rollout.

Most failed SCADA projects start with a plant-wide scope. We start with one asset and earn the next one.

01

Map the plant

List the machines, protocols, tag counts and who needs to see what. We do this with your team, on a call or on site.

02

Pilot one asset

Connect one machine or line, prove the data and the alarms, and measure whether the report saves anyone time.

03

Scale with a partner

Roll out through an authorised dealer or with our team, with licensing that matches how the plant buys.
