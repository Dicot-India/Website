# Refund & Cancellation Policy

Software licences and cloud subscriptions are digital products. This page explains when an order can be cancelled or refunded.

**Effective date:** 18 November 2026 · **Version:** 2.0

## 1\. Cancellation before delivery

If you cancel an order before a licence key is generated or a cloud subscription is activated, the order amount is refunded in full. Cancel by writing to [sales@dicot.tech](mailto:sales@dicot.tech) with the order or invoice number.

## 2\. After a licence is generated

A software licence cannot be cancelled or refunded once the licence key has been issued, because the product can be used immediately. This applies to Vision C, Vision S, VGraphic and cloud activations.

## 3\. Cloud subscriptions

Cloud subscriptions are paid in advance. Cancellation stops the next renewal; the current period is not refunded. If you cancel, export your data before the period ends. We retain cloud data as described in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/) and your order terms.

## 4\. Physical goods

If a physical item has shipped but has not been delivered, the order can be cancelled and the amount refunded after deducting shipping and handling, once the item is returned intact. Delivered items cannot be cancelled.

## 5\. Failed or duplicate transactions

If a payment fails but money is debited, or the same order is charged twice, the affected amount is refunded. Send the transaction reference and invoice number to [sales@dicot.tech](mailto:sales@dicot.tech). Bank or card charges may not be refundable.

## 6\. Processing time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days on our side. Your bank or card issuer may take longer to credit the amount. We cannot guarantee a specific credit date once the refund leaves us.

## 7\. Partial cancellations

Where an order includes multiple items and only some are cancelled, the refund corresponds to the cancelled part.

## 8\. Changes

We may update this policy. The version on this page applies to orders placed after its effective date.
