# Cookie Policy

We keep cookies to a minimum. Essential storage runs the site; analytics and support chat load only after you accept them, and we do not run advertising cookies.

**Effective date:** 18 November 2026 · **Version:** 2.0

## What we use

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration | Consent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| dicot\_consent\_v1 | Local storage | Remembers your cookie choice so we do not ask on every page | Until you clear browser storage | Essential |
| \_\_cf\_bm | Cookie | Cloudflare bot management and security | 30 minutes | Essential |
| \_ga | Cookie | Google Analytics 4 — distinguishes browsers for aggregate usage reporting | Up to 2 years | Analytics |
| \_ga\_<container> | Cookie | Google Analytics 4 — keeps session state | Up to 2 years | Analytics |
| \_\_tawkuuid | Cookie | Tawk.to live chat — recognises a returning chat visitor | Up to 6 months | Support chat |
| TawkConnectionTime | Cookie | Tawk.to live chat — manages the chat connection | Session | Support chat |
| tawk\_uuid\_<property> | Cookie | Tawk.to live chat — keeps the chat session across pages | Up to 6 months | Support chat |

We do not currently set advertising or marketing cookies. If that changes, we will add the category here and ask for consent before setting them.

## Third-party services

The partner application link opens a Google Form. Once you visit Google, Google’s cookies and privacy terms apply. Our fonts are self-hosted, so loading a page does not contact Google Fonts. The Tawk.to live-chat widget is loaded from Tawk.to only if you enable “Support chat” in the cookie preferences; its own cookies and privacy terms apply to the chat service. The contact form loads Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated spam; Cloudflare processes the verification token and IP address under its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## How to change your choice

Use the “Cookie settings” link in the footer to reopen the banner and change analytics, support-chat or marketing consent. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; blocking essential storage may make the consent banner reappear on each visit.

Rejecting analytics or chat does not restrict any part of the site. The contact form and downloads work without either.

## Do Not Track and AI crawlers

We do not use a separate Do Not Track response. Automated crawlers are governed by our [robots.txt](/robots.txt); we allow standard search engines and AI assistants to read public pages.

For the wider picture of how we handle personal data, see the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/) or write to [privacy@dicot.tech](mailto:privacy@dicot.tech).
