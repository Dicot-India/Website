Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025.

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Version: 2.0

Dicot Innovations Private Limited (“Dicot”, “we”, “us”) is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described in this policy. We are registered in India with our office at A-805, Siddhi Vinayak Towers, Off S. G. Road, B/H DCP Office, Makarba, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380051, India.

  • Privacy and data requests: privacy@dicot.tech

2. Scope

This policy covers personal data processed through dicot.tech and dicot.in, including our contact form, partner application link, careers applications, downloads, blog and analytics. Our customer platform at vision-web.tech may have a separate notice; where it does, that notice applies to that service.

This policy does not cover personal data in customer plant data that we process as a processor under a customer contract.

4. How we use personal data

  • Respond to enquiries, demos and partner applications.
  • Provide, licence, support and renew our software.
  • Send product updates where you have opted in.
  • Measure site usage where you have consented to analytics.
  • Protect the site, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use enquiry data for unrelated advertising.

5. Consent and withdrawal

Where we rely on consent, we ask for it separately and do not pre-tick the box. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to privacy@dicot.tech or by changing your cookie choices through “Cookie settings” in the footer. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and we will stop using the data for that purpose. You can also ask us to delete it, subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Cookies and analytics

Essential storage keeps your consent choice and the site working. Google Analytics 4 loads only after you accept analytics, and the Tawk.to chat widget loads only after you enable support chat. Neither loads if you reject or ignore the banner. See the cookie policy for the specific cookies and durations.

9. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, least-privilege accounts, patched infrastructure, backups and vendor contracts with confidentiality obligations. No system is completely secure; if you suspect a problem, contact privacy@dicot.tech immediately.

10. Data breaches

If a personal data breach occurs, we will contain it, assess the risk and notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals as required by the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules, 2025. We keep an internal record of breaches and remedial actions.

11. Your rights

As a Data Principal you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal data and provide a summary;
  • correct, complete or update inaccurate data;
  • erase data that is no longer needed for its purpose;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity;
  • withdraw consent; and
  • resolve a grievance.

Write to privacy@dicot.tech with the subject “DPDP request”. We may ask for information to verify your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days or the period prescribed under the DPDP Rules. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Children

Our website and products are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Marketing

We send product updates only where you opt in, and every message includes an unsubscribe route. You can also ask us to stop by emailing privacy@dicot.tech.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our processing changes and revise the effective date and version. Material changes will be communicated where required.
