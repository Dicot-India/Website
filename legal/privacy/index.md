## 2\. Scope

This policy covers personal data processed through **dicot.tech** and **dicot.in**, including our contact form, partner application link, careers applications, downloads, blog and analytics. We use this data to deliver the file, help with installation and keep the form from being misused. It is not used for unrelated advertising, and the marketing opt-in on that form is optional and separate.

## 5\. Consent and withdrawal

Where we rely on consent, we ask for it separately and do not pre-tick the box. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to [privacy@dicot.tech](mailto:privacy@dicot.tech) or by changing your cookie choices through “Cookie settings” in the footer. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and we will stop using the data for that purpose. You can also ask us to delete it, subject to legal retention requirements.

**Download consent.** The Vision S download form asks for a separate consent that is never pre-ticked. Every confirmation email repeats how to withdraw it, and a request to [privacy@dicot.tech](mailto:privacy@dicot.tech) with the subject “Withdraw download consent” is actioned without any further steps. Withdrawal does not affect the download you already received, and it is as easy to withdraw as it was to consent.

## 9\. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, least-privilege accounts, patched infrastructure, backups and vendor contracts with confidentiality obligations. No system is completely secure; if you suspect a problem, contact [privacy@dicot.tech](mailto:privacy@dicot.tech) immediately.

## 10\. Data breaches

If a personal data breach occurs, we will contain it, assess the risk and notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals as required by the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules, 2025. We keep an internal record of breaches and remedial actions.

## 11\. Your rights

As a Data Principal you can ask us to:

-   confirm whether we process your personal data and provide a summary;
-   correct, complete or update inaccurate data;
-   erase data that is no longer needed for its purpose;
-   nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity;
-   withdraw consent; and
-   resolve a grievance.

Write to [privacy@dicot.tech](mailto:privacy@dicot.tech) with the subject “DPDP request”. We may ask for information to verify your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days or the period prescribed under the DPDP Rules. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 12\. Children
